# RetailPulse: E-Commerce Customer Intelligence

## Project Overview
RetailPulse is a business analytics project built using the Instacart dataset containing grocery transactions.  

The goal of this project was to identify:
- customer churn patterns
- revenue leakage areas
- low reorder products
- customer engagement behavior
- retention opportunities

This project combines SQL, Python, and Power BI to simulate a real-world retail analytics workflow.

---

# Business Problem
Instacart operates one of the largest grocery delivery platforms with millions of transactions. Despite the scale, significant revenue is lost through:
- customer churn
- low reorder rates
- disengaged customer segments
- one-time product purchases

This analysis identifies where revenue leakage occurs and provides recommendations to improve customer retention and reorder behavior.

# Key Business Findings

## 1. Days Since Last Order is the Strongest Churn Predictor
Customers with longer gaps between orders are significantly more likely to churn.

## 2. 51.8% Customers are Low Engagement Users
Only 8.9% customers belong to the Champion segment, indicating a large retention opportunity.

## 3. Produce & Dairy Departments Have Highest Non-Reorder Counts
Fresh produce and dairy products generated the highest number of one-time purchases.

## 4. Cohort Retention Drops After Early Orders
Customer retention declines sharply after initial purchases.

## 5. Top Reordered Products are Fresh Produce
Products like:
- Banana
- Bag of Organic Bananas
- Organic Strawberries

showed the highest reorder frequency.

## 6. Monday 10AM is Peak Ordering Time
Customer ordering activity peaked during Monday mornings.

---

# Business Recommendations

## 1. Re-engagement Campaign for Inactive Customers
Target customers inactive for 15+ days using:
- push notifications
- discount offers
- reminder emails

This could reduce churn risk significantly.

## 2. Loyalty Program for Produce & Dairy
Introduce:
- subscription discounts
- bundle offers
- loyalty rewards

to improve repeat purchases in high leakage departments.

## 3. Champion Conversion Strategy
Focus retention efforts on Loyal Customers, as they are closest to becoming high-value Champion customers.
